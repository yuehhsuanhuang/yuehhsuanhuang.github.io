As an AI Intern at TVBS, Taiwan's leading media network, I was a key developer on a project to automate the news scheduling workflow. The system's primary goal was to codify a 20-year veteran editor's expertise into a scalable workflow using n8n and Google Gemini, successfully reducing the required editorial review time from 20 minutes to just 5 minutes — a 75% efficiency gain.

My contribution was realizing a data pipeline to integrate Google Trends keywords. After overcoming authentication challenges to deploy a custom Python script on Google Cloud Run, my solution replaced the default RSS link. This single improvement boosted the system's identification of hot topics by over 10x, directly enhancing the quality of data-driven journalism.

Furthermore, I contributed to the system’s long-term health by refining the Elasticsearch data schema for clarity, such as changing content_score to summary_score to more accurately reflect the data's origin. The project now processes over 2,000 articles monthly.
