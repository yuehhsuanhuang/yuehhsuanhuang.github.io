Mentor: [Prof. Che Lin](https://www.ee.ntu.edu.tw/profile1.php?teacher_id=25203){: .link-chip }

This project aims to address the efficiency bottleneck faced by advanced multimodal recommendation model -- [MTSTRec](https://icml.cc/virtual/2025/poster/43526){: .link-chip }. Although these models are powerful, their training cycles, which can take nearly a full day, make it challenging for them to rapidly adapt to the dynamic demands of real-world business. 

The core of my research is to explore how to develop a framework that enables quick adaptation of models to new datasets by leveraging transfer learning and parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques, such as LoRA. The overall time bottleneck of MTSTRec is the **process of generating high-quality product embeddings**. Currently, my work is focused on this aspect, aiming to propose a solution that balances efficiency and quality, thereby enhancing the real-world applicability of advanced models.
